# mavi-customer-journey-analysis-case-study
This repository contains a Business Analysis case study focused on analyzing the customer journey on Mavi's e-commerce website.

The analysis covers the customer journey from entering the website until successfully adding a product to the shopping cart.

## Objectives

- Analyze the customer journey and user flow
- Create a detailed customer flowchart
- Identify strengths and improvement opportunities
- Evaluate the experience from both customer and business perspectives
- Suggest possible improvements and business KPIs

## Scope

The analysis covers the following journey:

Homepage → Category/Search → Product Listing → Product Detail → Size Selection → Add to Cart

Checkout, payment, and order confirmation are outside the scope of this analysis.

## Contents

### Customer Journey Flowchart

A detailed flowchart was created to visualize the customer journey, including:

- User actions
- System responses
- Decision points
- Alternative paths

### Customer Perspective

The analysis focuses on the main customer expectations during the shopping journey, including:

- Finding products easily
- Comparing options
- Accessing clear product information
- Selecting the correct size
- Adding products to the cart without friction

### Strengths

Some of the positive findings identified during the analysis include:

- Clear and intuitive navigation
- High-quality product presentation
- Detailed and understandable filters
- Clear product, payment, and return information
- Visible add-to-cart confirmation
- Clear cart information
- Helpful AI Assistant

### Improvement Opportunities

The analysis also identifies several potential improvement areas, such as:

- Reducing visual competition on the homepage
- Improving navigation and product discovery
- Simplifying some areas of the user journey

## Business Perspective

The case study connects user experience observations with potential business impact.

Suggested metrics include:

- Add-to-Cart Rate
- Conversion Rate
- Product Page Exit Rate
- Filter Usage Rate
- Customer Satisfaction

## Files

- Presentation: Customer Journey Analysis
- Excel: Data Analysis Case Study
- Flowchart: Detailed Customer Journey Flow

## Note

This project was prepared as part of a Business Analyst case study.
